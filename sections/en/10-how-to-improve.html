HOW TO IMPROVE AT:

Mythic+ in general

1. Knowing dungeon mechanics is huge.
2. Use addons that provide you with useful information.
3. If you can, find a steady group of players to run dungeons with.
4. Be patient. Acting like an elitist and being an asshole will just waste your time and other people's time, especially if you suck at the game.
(If you're being an asshole and every group you're in fails, you're probably the one who sucks)

Class/spec/role

1. Read a guide to see if you're doing anything fundamentally wrong.
2. Always be casting.
3. Use your cooldowns.
4. Compare logs and talent builds with players of similar item levels who may be outperforming you.

The most important things that lead to doing well in Mythic+ (and the game in general) are these two things:

1. Know the dungeon

BOSS MECHANICS
You need to know what bosses do, and what can go wrong.

TRASH MECHANICS
You need to know what trash can wipe you.
What spells MUST be interrupted? What trash should be stunned?
What is going to do a lot of damage to your group?
This sounds like a lot, but it all comes with time. Run the same dungeon 3-4 times and you'll pretty much know all there is to know.

2. Know your class

If you feel like you're plateauing or you're underperforming, there are a few simple things to remember:

ALWAYS BE CASTING
If you aren't casting or using abilities, you aren't doing damage or healing.

USE YOUR COOLDOWNS
Like I said above, it's almost always better to use your cooldowns than sit on them forever. Just don't use them if the pack you're fighting is almost dead.

USE YOUR DEFENSIVE AND INTERRUPT
Can't do damage if you're dead.

EXTRA CREDIT
Use the Warcraft Logs client to upload your logs and compare yourself to other people who are performing better at the same item level. See what they're doing differently.

There's no shortcut to being better at the game. Eventually you'll stop feeling overwhelmed with information and things will make sense.
