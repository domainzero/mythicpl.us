About mythicpl.us

What is this?

mythicpl.us is a quick-reference site for World of Warcraft Mythic+ dungeons. Check this week's affixes, loot tables, Great Vault rewards, and more.

Who makes this?

Just one person. Hi.

Why?

I wanted a fast, no-nonsense page to check affixes without wading through pop-ups and autoplaying videos.

Data sources

Affix data comes from Raider.io's API. Blue post data comes from the official Blizzard forums.

Open source

The source code is on GitHub. Contributions and suggestions are welcome.
